ATL here is the lowest price since the coin listed on Binance (USDT pair), read from weekly candles. That can be above the coin's true all-time low on other exchanges. The listing week and flash-crash wicks more than 50% below a week's open or close are ignored. Tap a coin for its chart, a liquidation calculator and the order book near its ATL.

Research tool for personal use. Data is read straight from Binance's public API in your browser (and the coin list from CoinGecko). Nothing is stored or sent anywhere else. The liquidation figures use Binance's simplified isolated-margin formula and ignore fees, funding and tiered margin, so treat them as estimates. A coin returning to its ATL often breaks it. This is research, not financial advice.
