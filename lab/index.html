1 · What big orders do around bottoms

A bottom is the lowest price within ±14 days, at least 30% below the prior 90-day high, followed by a 25% rally. Each bottom is compared with a falling knife on the same coin (also down 30%, but price kept falling at least another 10%) and with a random day. Every measure is shown relative to that coin's normal level. "Big orders" are taker orders of $100k or more.

Each bar is the rank correlation between a measure on a possible-bottom day and the return over the next 72 hours: 0 means no link, ±0.1 would already be useful. Measures that looked promising in 2022–23, like funding and taker buying, faded to nothing afterwards. Only volume vs normal and depth of the drawdown stayed slightly positive in both periods, which matches the spot finding that real bottoms come with a volume burst.
